TallyTouch

A fast, glanceable tally counter you can run without looking. Tap the big button or click the volume keys — feel every count.

Privacy Policy Support

What it is

Tap anywhere on the big number to count up. Or turn on eyes-free mode and click the hardware volume keys instead — every press lands with a crisp haptic tap, so you can keep your eyes on what you are actually counting. Reps, laps, boxes, people, stitches.

Built for speed

The whole screen is the button. The on-screen counter works fully on its own; the volume-key mode is an option, not a requirement. A firmer pulse marks each milestone.

More than one counter

Keep as many named counters as you like, each with its own step size, optional limits, milestone interval, and goal. Switch the active one in a tap.

See your pace

A History tab with today's total, a seven-day chart, and your peak counts-per-minute. A Lock Screen Live Activity, a Home Screen widget, and a standalone Apple Watch counter.

Private by design

Zero data leaves your device. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The optional volume-key mode reads only the system volume level — it never records audio and needs no microphone permission.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

The short version TallyTouch collects nothing. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Every count stays on your device.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the TallyTouch iOS app (the "App"), published by Ilia Filiaev (the "Developer"), handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The App does not have user accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not communicate with any server controlled by the Developer or any third party.

Data the App stores on your device

  • Counters and counts. Your counters and their change history are stored on your device using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device and is shared only with the App's own Home Screen widget through an App Group container.
  • Preferences. Settings such as haptics and the default milestone interval are stored locally.

The optional volume-button mode

If you enable eyes-free counting, the App observes the system output volume level to detect hardware volume-key presses. It reads only the volume level — it does not record, request, or access microphone audio, and the App requests no microphone permission of any kind.

Permissions the App requests

None. TallyTouch requires no special permissions to run.

Third parties

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, there is nothing to access, export, or delete. You can remove all on-device data by deleting the App from your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be announced in the App release notes.

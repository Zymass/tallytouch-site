Support

We are happy to help. Most answers are below.

How do I count?

Open the Count tab and tap anywhere on the big number to count up. Use the minus button to count down, the curved arrow to undo, and the circular arrow to reset.

How does eyes-free counting work?

Tap the speaker icon on the Count tab (or enable it in Settings) to count with the hardware volume keys. Volume Up counts up, Volume Down counts down, and each press is confirmed with a haptic tap. It reads only the volume level — it never records audio.

Can I keep several counters?

Yes. On the Counters tab, tap the plus button to add a counter and give it a name, step size, optional minimum and maximum, a milestone interval, and a goal. Tap a counter to make it the active one shown on the Count tab and in the widget.

Where do I see my pace?

The History tab shows today's total, a seven-day chart, and your peak counts-per-minute.

Is my data backed up to the cloud?

No. All data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes everything.
